HEADLINE: Analog Devices to Acquire Empower Semiconductor, Expanding its Next-Generation High-Density Power Portfolio for the AI Era
CATEGORY: COMPANY
IMPACT SCORE: 5/5
FINANCIAL STATEMENT LINE(S): cash and cash equivalents, cash flow from investing activities, goodwill, intangible assets, revenue/sales, COGS, R&D expense, SG&A expense, transaction and integration expense, operating income, EPS, working capital
WHY IT COULD AFFECT EARNINGS: ADI agreed to pay $1.5 billion in cash for Empower, making the transaction unusually consequential for investing cash flow and the post-close balance sheet, including acquired intangible assets and goodwill. Empower’s integrated-voltage-regulator and silicon-capacitor products are already in production and its programs are advancing with hyperscalers and AI-silicon providers, creating a concrete route to incremental data-center power revenue and mix after closing. Transaction, integration and retention costs can initially increase operating expenses and dilute EPS; timing remains subject to closing conditions.
CONTENT (Summary from accessible article): ADI and Empower Semiconductor entered a definitive agreement under which ADI will acquire Empower for $1.5 billion in cash. ADI said Empower’s silicon capacitors are already in production and its integrated-voltage-regulator programs are advancing with leading hyperscalers and AI-silicon providers. The transaction was expected to close in the second half of calendar 2026, subject to customary conditions and Hart-Scott-Rodino review.

ADI described the expansion as part of its hybrid internal-factory, foundry and OSAT manufacturing strategy.
