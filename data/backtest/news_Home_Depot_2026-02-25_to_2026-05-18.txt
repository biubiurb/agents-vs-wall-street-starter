HEADLINE: U.S. strengthens aluminum, steel and copper tariff regimes, including 50% duties for covered products
CATEGORY: MACRO
IMPACT SCORE: 4/5
FINANCIAL STATEMENT LINE(S): revenue/sales, COGS, gross margin, operating expense, working capital, cash flow, EPS
WHY IT COULD AFFECT EARNINGS: The proclamation increased or broadened duties on covered aluminum, steel, copper and derivative goods effective April 6. These materials and products are relevant to Home Depot’s tools, hardware, building-material and appliance assortment; higher supplier or direct-import costs can raise COGS, pressure margin if not passed through, alter pricing and demand, and require more inventory working capital.
CONTENT (Summary from accessible article): The White House proclamation applied duties to the full customs value of covered imports beginning April 6, 2026. It set a 50% additional ad valorem duty for many listed aluminum, steel, copper and derivative articles, with specified lower rates and exclusions for certain products.
