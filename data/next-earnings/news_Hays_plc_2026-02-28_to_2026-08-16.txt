HEADLINE: Hays announces reshaping of country portfolio
CATEGORY: COMPANY
IMPACT SCORE: 4/5
FINANCIAL STATEMENT LINE(S): revenue/sales, operating profit, impairment, cash flow, working capital, cash
WHY IT COULD AFFECT EARNINGS: The completed sale removes six country operations from future reported revenue and profit, generates approximately £4m of net cash proceeds after costs and working-capital items, and creates a non-cash loss on sale. Hays also identified a further seven countries under strategic review; the 13 countries collectively generated about £85m of FY26 net fees but broadly break-even pre-exceptional operating profit, making the portfolio change material to reported sales mix, cash flow and exceptional charges.
CONTENT (Summary from accessible article): Hays completed the sale of its Czech Republic, Denmark, Hungary, Luxembourg, Romania and Sweden operations to Meraki Capital on 16 June for approximately £4m of net cash proceeds after transaction costs, working-capital and other items. It expected a modest non-cash loss on disposal in H2 FY26. Hays was also exploring options for Belgium, Brazil, Greater China, Malaysia, the Netherlands, Singapore and the UAE, and said the 13 countries together were expected to generate about £85m of FY26 net fees with broadly break-even pre-exceptional operating profit.

Hays derives a substantial share of group net fees from Temp & Contracting and remains exposed to UK&I, so the sector shift is material to placement volume and revenue mix. It attributed the preference for temporary staff to geopolitical uncertainty and cost considerations. Overall staff demand nevertheless fell at the quickest pace since January, mainly from lower permanent vacancies, while starting salaries and temporary wages rose faster.
